How these figures were produced, and what was left out
  • A country appears only if it has both a reported case count and a population figure for that exact year. A country that did not report is excluded rather than recorded as zero — no report is not the same as no cases.
  • Cases per 100,000 is cases × 100000 / population, using that country's population in that year rather than a fixed recent figure.
  • Nine country codes appear in the vaccination records with no matching entry in the country reference table. They are excluded by the joins rather than shown with a blank name.
  • The pooled rate in the summary is SUM(cases) / SUM(population). Averaging the individual country rates instead would give a country of 90,000 people the same weight as one of 1.4 billion.
