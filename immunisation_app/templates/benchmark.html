What “global” means here

The benchmark includes {{ result.global.countries }} countries and territories with numeric case data and positive population for this year. {{ result.excluded }} reported records are excluded because their cases, population or country match cannot support a rate.

Every comparable country contributes to the global benchmark, including countries with reported zero cases and those without an economic classification. This is not an unweighted average of country rates. Comparisons use unrounded values; displayed figures are rounded.
