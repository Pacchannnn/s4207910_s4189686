Data notes: what the terms mean

Missing data is not zero. Each analysis page explains which records its results represent.

Antigen
The vaccine or dose category selected for the vaccination analysis.
Vaccination coverage
The reported coverage percentage, with a fallback of valid doses divided by a positive target count, multiplied by 100. Missing doses do not provide a fallback.
Recorded doses and population-based rate
Doses are the recorded administered doses. Improvement compares doses divided by the population in the same year, multiplied by 100. This uses population, not the coverage target. Both years require valid data.
Percentage points (pp)
The end-year rate minus the start-year rate. This is how vaccination improvement is expressed.
Infection rate
Reported cases divided by the matching same-year population, multiplied by 100,000: cases per 100,000 people.
Global benchmark
Total reported cases divided by total represented population, multiplied by 100,000. It is weighted, not a simple average of country rates, and represents valid matching records rather than the entire world population.
