Mission and audience

Evidence that informs without overstating.

Our purpose is to help people investigate the impact of preventable infectious diseases through transparent, comparable and respectfully presented data.

How to use the site

Three layers of understanding

The site supports quick orientation and deeper investigation without presenting association as causation.

  1. 1

    Orient

    Start on this mission page to see the purpose of the site, the people it is built for and the team behind it.

  2. 2

    Focus

    Use the infection explorer to compare reported cases and rates for one economic status, infection type and year.

  3. 3

    Deepen

    Place countries against a weighted global infection rate to see which report above the global average.
