How this is calculated

Doses ÷ population × 100

For each country, subtract the start-year rate from the end-year rate. Results show the increase in percentage points. Each endpoint uses that year's population.

This is a population-based administered-dose rate, as required by Level 3A. It is different from target-group vaccination coverage and does not count unique vaccinated people.
